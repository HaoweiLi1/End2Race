# PPO V1

V1 completed 20 updates with 12,800 transitions and 16 minibatches per update. Evaluation used the fixed 600-case Austin panel with ego collision scope.

The selected checkpoint was update 5: 17 ego collisions, 236 follow, and 347 overtake. The paired BC result was 21/233/346, and the overtake eligibility floor was 329.

The retained files are the resolved configuration, selection decision, and 20 update-level training metric records.
